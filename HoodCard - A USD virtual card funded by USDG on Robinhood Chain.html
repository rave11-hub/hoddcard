How it works

A licensed issuing partner issues the card. You run the vault and the authorization decisions.

DepositSign in with a wallet (no password) and send USDG to the vault on Robinhood Chain.
IssueThe issuer runs a one-time identity check in its hosted flow, then the card is created.
AuthorizeThe issuer sends a webhook and the backend checks the balance.
SettleThe merchant is paid in USD and the vault is debited in USDG.
